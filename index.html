Selected Work 01

18-DOF Hexapod

View Code & Math →

A team of four is building a spider-inspired 18-DOF hexapod designed to climb stairs autonomously. I wrote the PyBullet simulation (inverse kinematics, Wave/Tripod/Ripple gaits, servo torque analysis) and am developing the Raspberry Pi control software and operator dashboard. Status: robot assembled and in bring-up; running the simulated gaits on the hardware is the next milestone.
